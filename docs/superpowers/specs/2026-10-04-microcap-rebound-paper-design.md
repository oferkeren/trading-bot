# TradingMax news-driven micro-cap rebound strategy

## Scope and intent

Build a long-only, single-symbol, repeatable rebound strategy for **IBKR paper trading only**. It selects from the current top gainers in premarket, regular, or after-hours trading, verifies fresh company-specific news, then waits for a statistically favorable low rather than buying solely because a stock dropped or has news. After a protected exit it can wait for another setup in the same symbol. It abandons that symbol when the catalyst or measurable rebound edge disappears. No live activation is part of this project.

The strategy estimates conditional outcomes; it does not claim to locate exact tops/bottoms or guarantee a $0.50-$2.00 per-share profit. Those amounts are candidate targets, not mandatory targets.

## Boundaries and data flow

1. **Candidate discovery:** Use IBKR entitled real-time market data to rank *current*, not future, gainers within each session. Normalize symbol identity and exclude unsupported contracts. Persist the observation time and session. Rank only symbols for which current quotes and session data are actually available. A delayed or frozen quote does not qualify.
2. **Catalyst gate:** Obtain free timestamped news with provider, publication time, symbol/company identity, headline, and article identifier. Reuse the existing `news_context.py` subject checks where appropriate. Deduplicate syndicated/recycled stories; score company relevance, sentiment, and potential impact. Require publication within the last 48 hours **and** a current price/volume reaction; record news age and whether the catalyst remains active. Irrelevant, missing, unverified, stale, or ambiguously timed news blocks entry. Do not infer news from a price spike. Confirm actual API coverage before enabling paper entries; if free historical news cannot support validation, report that limitation and do not invent probabilities or relax this gate.
3. **Historical research:** Source multi-year historical price/volume bars from Alpaca's available free feed. Record feed, timestamp, session, adjustment policy, symbol mapping, and coverage gaps; handle splits, corporate actions, delistings, and missing bars explicitly. Use daily/hourly history for context and 1-minute/5-minute bars where available for comparable intraday setups. Historical catalyst labels must be timestamped and known by each historical decision time. If a feature, label, quote, or spread is unavailable, do not impute future data. Avoid treating today's winners as an unbiased historical universe: current-day candidates can be researched, while strategy-level performance claims require an as-of historical candidate replay with losers and failed catalysts included.
4. **Statistical model:** Define a completed swing using only information available at the decision time (a confirmed low, rise, then subsequent confirmed low); select its amplitude/time thresholds on earlier training data, not on the event being evaluated. Condition comparable outcomes on session, price band, volatility, drawdown, volume, VWAP distance, liquidity/spread, and catalyst age/type when reliably recorded. For each potential entry and target, estimate target-before-stop probability, expected time, net per-share outcome, failure rate, and uncertainty after observed or conservative spread, slippage, and fees. Stops are evaluated before targets on ambiguous bars. Evaluate +$0.50, +$1.00, +$1.50, and +$2.00 as well as data-supported intermediate targets; never select one merely because its gross return is largest.
5. **Live setup/exit evaluator:** Use fresh IBKR live 1-minute/5-minute and bid/ask data for the current candidate. Require a confirmed low and evidence of improving selling pressure, acceptable spread/liquidity, active volume/volatility, and resemblance to historical successful setups. Produce a buy zone, entry limit, invalidation/stop, candidate target distribution, and selected target. Choose the target with the best conservative *net* tradeoff; if no target meets the calibrated gates, return `NO_TRADE` with reasons. Re-evaluate suitability after every completed trade, never chase an unfilled entry.
6. **Single-symbol paper controller:** Persist symbol ownership and state across restarts: `SELECT`, `WAIT_FOR_LOW`, `ENTER`, `MANAGE_EXIT`, `WAIT_FOR_NEXT_LOW`, `RETIRE`. Retain ownership until the position and its orders are reconciled. Serialize entry claims within TradingMax and check a fresh account-wide broker snapshot of positions and open/pending orders immediately before submission; any conflicting exposure blocks a new entry or symbol switch, including exposure initiated outside this strategy. External actors can still race with that snapshot, so reconcile again after submission and block further entries on conflict. Reconcile fills, partial fills, cancellations, and broker identity before resuming after a restart. Use existing order/protection infrastructure rather than creating a second unguarded order path.

## Risk and failure behavior

All orders use the IBKR **paper** account/connection, with the existing execution-mode guard preventing a route to live. Entries have an attached protective stop and target; broker protection and reconciliation remain authoritative. Position size and aggregate loss exposure inherit existing TradingMax per-trade and daily limits, and research may only tighten them. No unlimited trades or escalation of limits based on favorable statistics. Validate entitlement, quote freshness, market session, news, account state, order state, limits, liquidity, and effective stop before entry; any failure blocks new entries and reports a specific reason. Existing protection is never silently removed to recover from stale data. Alert on unprotected exposure or irreconcilable broker state. Stop re-entry when news expires, price/volume reaction fades, spreads widen, rebound amplitude drops below costs, losses accumulate, or current behavior leaves the calibrated domain; return to selection only once broker state is flat and unambiguous.

The paper strategy can place orders automatically once all gates pass; there is no per-order human approval. Calibration parameters and model versions must be recorded and frozen before that happens. Research does not override existing safety controls or activate live execution.

## Candidate output

For each evaluated symbol report: symbol and session; source, timestamp, headline, age, relevance, sentiment, impact and active status of the catalyst; current price, buy zone, entry and expected rebound; estimated target-before-stop probabilities for +$0.50, +$1.00, +$1.50 and +$2.00 (or `unavailable` with reason); selected target, expected time, invalidation/stop, spread, liquidity status, confidence interval and sample size; data/feed freshness, model version, current controller state, repeat-suitability, and explicit `BUY_ELIGIBLE`/`NO_TRADE` reasons. Probabilities are conditional estimates, not guarantees.

## Validation and activation

Work proceeds in order: establish free-data coverage and point-in-time timestamps; develop historical labels and replay; calibrate and freeze parameters on training data; evaluate chronological out-of-sample periods and compare with no-trade and simple rebound baselines; then connect the IBKR live-data evaluator to automatic **paper-only** execution. The testing harness must cover delisted/missing symbols, wrong-company/duplicate/stale news, unavailable news history, extended-hours gaps, delayed IBKR quotes, stale bars, splits, ambiguous stop/target ordering, fees and slippage, insufficient samples, broker disconnects, partial fills, concurrent entries, cross-bot exposure, restarts, and stop protection.

Paper activation requires a reproducible report with actual data coverage, independent event counts, chronological holdout performance, probability calibration, net outcomes after costs, drawdowns, and a sensitivity analysis of fill assumptions. Predeclare sample-size and conservative confidence thresholds before evaluating the final holdout; if data are too sparse to validate them, remain in analysis mode and report `NO_TRADE`. Monitor paper fills versus modeled fills and disable new entries when the discrepancy or data quality invalidates the model. Live trading would require a separate design and explicit approval.

## Existing integration points

Candidate discovery extends `scanner.py`; news validation extends `news_context.py` and existing AI context only as evidence, never as a substitute for verified timestamps; historical analysis remains separate from live order routing; setup timing integrates with `strategy_engine.py`; IBKR paper entries reuse `worker_core.py`, `risk_engine.py`, `position_policy.py`, exposure/protection guards, and `trade_monitor_core.py`. Preserve the repository's fail-closed behavior. Existing working-tree changes to these modules are outside this spec commit.
